# Design System: RentNaija

A trustworthy estate-office interface: warm paper surfaces, one deep palm-green accent, quiet type. Mobile-first for Nigerian renters. Density balanced; listing grids regular; motion restrained.

## Colors
- Warm Paper #F6F1E8 page canvas
- Clean Surface #FFFCF7 cards and forms
- Charcoal Ink #1C1917 primary text (never pure black)
- Muted Clay #78716C secondary text
- Hairline Stone #E7E5E4 borders
- Palm Trust #1B6B4A single accent for buttons, links, focus, available chips
- Palm Soft #E8F3EC selected filters and success tints
- Laterite Alert #B42318 errors only

## Type
Plus Jakarta Sans for headlines, body, and labels. Tabular/mono figures for Naira prices. No Inter. No generic serifs.

## Components
Buttons 8px radius, 44px min height. Primary filled accent, secondary outlined. WhatsApp green only on that one contact button. Cards 12px radius, 1px hairline, whisper shadow. Inputs: label above, error below, no floating labels. Status chips are pills (Pending, Approved, Rented). Filters use 8px radius. Skeleton loaders matching layout. Empty states with one primary action. Compact top bar wordmark RentNaija; mobile bottom bar Home, Search, Saved, Dashboard.

## Layout
Single column below 768px. Max width 1200px. Home: left-aligned headline and stacked search (location, budget, Find homes); featured listings as horizontal snap row; popular locations 2-up on mobile, not three equal marketing cards. Listings: sticky sort plus Filters drawer on mobile, sidebar from 1024px. Property details: gallery first then price and contact; sticky contact aside on desktop. Dashboards list-first, no invented KPIs.

## Content
Nigerian names and areas: Lekki, Wuse 2, Trans Amadi, Bodija, New Haven. Prices in Naira per year. No fake metrics, no emojis in chrome, no scroll-to-explore, no overlapping hero text without a solid panel.
